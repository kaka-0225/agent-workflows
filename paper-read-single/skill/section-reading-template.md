# Section Reading Template

## Task

Read one paper section in depth.

## Expected Output

```md
# Section Reading Notes

## Section

## Purpose In The Paper

## Key Points

## Important Terms

## Evidence-Backed Claims

| Claim | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|

## Interpretation

## Uncertain Points

## Follow-Up Questions
```

## Rules

- Do not summarize unrelated sections unless needed for context.
- Mark missing or ambiguous evidence.
