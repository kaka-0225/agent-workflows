# Personal Reflection Template

## Task

Connect one book idea to the reader's provided context.

## Expected Output

```md
# Personal Reflection

## Book Idea

## User-Provided Context

## What The Book May Be Pointing At

## Gentle Interpretation

## Possible Patterns, Stated Cautiously

## Questions To Sit With

## Small Experiments Or Practices

## Boundaries

- Not a diagnosis:
- What remains uncertain:
- When to seek human support:
```

## Rules

- Do not diagnose.
- Do not claim certainty about motives or history.
- Keep the tone respectful, spacious, and non-prescriptive.
