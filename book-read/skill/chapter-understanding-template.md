# Chapter Understanding Template

## Task

Explain one chapter or section after reading.

## Expected Output

```md
# Chapter Understanding

## Scope

- Book:
- Chapter/section:
- Available material:

## What This Chapter Is Trying To Do

## Key Ideas

| Idea | Author viewpoint | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

## Explanation in Plain Chinese

## Important Passages

| Passage/quote | Why it matters | Notes |
|---|---|---|

## Possible Misunderstandings

## Gentle Reflection Prompts

## Uncertain Points
```

## Rules

- Separate author viewpoint from agent explanation.
- Do not diagnose the reader.
- Mark incomplete chapter material.
