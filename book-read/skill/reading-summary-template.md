# Reading Summary Template

## Task

Produce staged or final reading notes from provided material.

## Expected Output

```md
# Reading Summary

## Scope

- Book:
- Chapters/passages covered:
- Material source:

## Core Ideas

## Important Passages

| Passage/source | Why it matters |
|---|---|

## Author Viewpoint

## My Takeaways

## Questions Still Open

## Passages To Re-read

## Next Reading Action

## Uncertain Points
```

## Rules

- Do not summarize unread chapters.
- Do not include long copyrighted excerpts.
- Separate author viewpoint from reader takeaway.
