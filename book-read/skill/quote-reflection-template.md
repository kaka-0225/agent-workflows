# Quote Reflection Template

## Task

Reflect on one quote or short passage.

## Expected Output

```md
# Quote Reflection

## Quote or Passage

## Literal Meaning

## Possible Deeper Meaning

## Emotional or Philosophical Tension

## Context If Known

## How It Might Apply In Real Life

## Reflection Questions

## Boundaries and Uncertainty
```

## Rules

- Keep the quote's ambiguity when present.
- Do not overgeneralize from one quote.
- Do not diagnose the reader.
